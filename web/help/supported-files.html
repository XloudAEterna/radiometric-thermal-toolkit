Supported input
  • JPEG files with the .jpg or .jpeg extension.
  • DJI radiometric R-JPEG files containing temperature data.
  • Files with _R or _T suffixes and unclassified JPEG files are checked for conversion.
Files not converted
  • _V visible-light images are identified and skipped.
  • _Z zoom images are identified and skipped.
  • Recompressed or modified JPEG files may no longer contain radiometric temperature data.
Thermal matrix dimensions
  • Thermal dimensions are read dynamically from the DJI Thermal SDK.
  • Common matrix sizes include 640 × 512 and 1280 × 1024.
  • Preview image dimensions are not used when they differ from the thermal matrix.
Tested DJI devices

H20T, H20N, H30T, Mavic 2 Enterprise Advanced, Mavic 3 Thermal, Mavic 3TD, Matrice 4T, M30T and Zenmuse XT S.
